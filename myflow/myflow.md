

how would i want to start the presentation on "Agent Skills" to a large audience of 350 people in just 10 minutes?

i would want to start with explaining that the models are great, they can give us knowledge that it was trained on, but they have a hard time with multi-step processes. They can give us the "what" but not the "how". 



Then I would introduce the concept of "Agent Skills" as a way to fill that gap. I would explain that a skill is essentially a markdown file that contains step-by-step instructions for a specific task. This allows the agent to perform complex workflows without needing to be prompted with every single step every time.
